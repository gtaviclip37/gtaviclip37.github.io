klipy-upload Privacy Policy Terms of Service
Uploads my own videos to YouTube using YouTube API Services

A personal, non-commercial tool. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

klipy-upload is a small desktop script used only by its owner to upload his own edited short gaming clips from his own computer to his own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. It is not offered to, sold to, or used by anyone else. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Terms of Service

  • The tool is used exclusively by its owner for his own accounts.
  • Videos are uploaded only after the owner explicitly approves each upload.
  • By authorizing the tool, the account owner allows it to upload videos and read basic account information on his request. Authorization can be revoked at any time.
  • By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's data practices: Google Privacy Policy.
  • The tool complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service, the Instagram Platform Terms and the TikTok Developer Terms. The tool is provided "as is", without warranties.

Privacy (summary)

  • Only the owner's own account data (name, ID) is accessed; no data about other users.
  • Tokens are stored only locally on the owner's computer; nothing is shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Full details: Privacy Policy.
